How to use

Draw a route

Click the map to drop points. Each new point is joined to the last one along real roads and paths.

Press and hold on the map for about half a second - a green ring fills, then a point drops. Quick taps, swipes and pinches only move the map, so you can scroll around freely.

The top panel shows distance, walking time and running time as you go. Set your own pace to get an accurate running time.

Reshape it

Drag any dot to move it, or drag the line itself to pull out a new bend.

Drag any dot to move it. To pull the line out into a new bend, press and hold on the line until the ring fires, then drag.

Tap a dot to see its address or delete it - the route re-joins around the gap.

Roads or free draw

Follow Roads snaps each leg to streets. Switch it off for straight lines through parks and trails.

Freehand Draw lets you trace a path with your finger or mouse. Map dragging is paused while it's on.

Location not working?

Routr starts where you are if you allow location. Click or tap the ◎ button (bottom-right) to jump to yourself any time.

iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → turn it on, then Safari Websites → While Using the App. Reload the page.

Android: tap the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → Location → Allow. Reload the page.

If we can't find you, the map starts at Times Square.

More menu

  • Undo removes your last action.
  • Add Current Location drops a point where you are.
  • Suggest a Route finds loops near your start for a distance you choose.
  • Reorder Points changes the order the route visits them.
  • Close Loop joins the last point back to the first.
  • Directions lists the turns and street names.
  • Elevation shows hills, total climb and a difficulty rating.
  • My Routes saves and reloads routes on this device.
  • Export GPX downloads the route for a watch or other app.
  • Clear removes the whole route.
